## Source and known-issue boundary

HBase 2.6.6 source (`/users/ZhenyuLi/.graphchecker-sources/hbase-2.6.6`, `d5f63f523c`) shows why the schedule decides the outcome: `ServerStateNode.java:38-39,90-96` uses one `ReentrantReadWriteLock`; `AssignmentManager.java:1256-1278` holds its READ through transition processing and `:1975-2009` must acquire WRITE before submitting SCP. `RegionRemoteProcedureBase.java:287-295,322-346` holds the region lock while synchronously persisting to meta; `RegionStateStore.java:231-249` calls `HTable.put`. In f001/f002 the RPC READ holder waited for that region lock, its PEWorker owner was retrying meta I/O to the failed host, and the tracker could not acquire WRITE to start meta-host SCP. In f003 no READ remained when WRITE was requested, so the same recovery path advanced. The negative control supports a **timing-dependent pre-SCP wait**, not a deterministic effect of the two injected exceptions alone.

The two positive runs prove bounded 600 s noncompletion, not an infinite deadlock: f002's meta client had reached only retry 36 of 46 when the harness timed out. [HBASE-24526](https://issues.apache.org/jira/browse/HBASE-24526?focusedCommentId=17149647) remains an open, broad meta-update/PEWorker nonprogress overlap, but its documented meta assign is already scheduled. [HBASE-29259 comment 17943805](https://issues.apache.org/jira/browse/HBASE-29259?focusedCommentId=17943805) describes a user-region meta update waiting for SCP to assign meta; its primary reported bug is an orphaned procedure on master restart with SCP/TRSP already concurrent. Neither source establishes the exact pre-SCP READ/WRITE wait as a known duplicate or proves its novelty. The branch-2.6 snapshot at `03be5bee0ade1072258189bc9e28c12f193bab4b` still contains synchronous meta persistence and the fence; current master uses an async future and needs separate runtime evaluation.

**Independent verdict:** the owner's classification is consistent with the raw evidence: `scheduling_sensitive_recovery_wait_candidate`, `new_bug=false`. f003 is a valid negative control and does not invalidate the two positive bounded waits. A new-bug claim needs a same-plan causal differential or additional post-retry recovery evidence, plus a sharper comparison with HBASE-24526 and HBASE-29259. The direct B7 rethrow remains expected under injection.
