## Evidence and limits

- Local Solr raw receipts and raw-event audits: `cases/SOLR-01/independent/evidence/campaign/`, `cases/SOLR-05/evidence/campaign/`, `cases/SOLR-06/independent/evidence/campaign/`, `cases/SOLR-07/independent/evidence/campaign/`, `cases/SOLR-10/work/evidence/campaign/`, and `cases/SOLR-19/independent/evidence/campaign/`. Timeout trials can retain the target yet lack a JaCoCo file; production coverage summaries exclude them (8 for SOLR-05, 1 for SOLR-06, 7 for SOLR-07, 7 for SOLR-19). Each has an event trace and measured time.
- SOLR-18 raw traces and JaCoCo files remain at `/users/ZhenyuLi/solr18-independent-runs`; all 124 were present and matched the case-local ledger's strict and selected original-test-body outcomes. SOLR-15/21/31/34 source raw traces remain on node2. On 2026-10-07, these five completed cases were copied into compressed case-local archives on node0. The archive integrity and every trial event/JaCoCo digest were independently verified against the pre-archive receipts; see `archived-solr-raw-2026-10-07.json`. These archives preserve the audited evidence even if the external source directories are later removed.
- SOLR-15 exact candidate and replay: `cases/SOLR-14/independent/supplemental/shardsplit/solr15-native-candidate-source-review.json`, plus `native-chaos-fault-02/audit.json` and `native-chaos-replay-01/audit.json`. Both apply two source/physical-node-matched ZooKeeper faults, reach the line-538 throw after the second, and pass the original chaos JUnit. The source marks child shards recovery-failed and aborts the split when the parent leader's live znode disappears. This is an expected abort for that test, not a confirmed new bug. The SOLR-15 result combines shared campaigns and supplemental JUnit invocations; its `fault_trials=163` must not be read as 163 target-retaining trials.
- All 11 ZooKeeper raw archives matched their recorded SHA-256 values. The `.tar.gz` cases contain original healthy/trial JUnit metadata, event and coverage TSVs; the `.tar.zst` cases have case-local provenance/trace manifests. ZOOKEEPER-13 archive contains 128 completed trials plus five archived invalid orchestration attempts, which are excluded from its budget. ZooKeeper coverage is JaCoCo production probe/line feedback; ZOOKEEPER-13's two named new branch outcomes are source-reviewed exclusive line bodies, not general branch-probe coverage.
- The absent `target_reached` field in ZOOKEEPER-05 is a result-schema inconsistency only: its healthy provenance has six exact guard hits, the raw trial ledger records 35 admitted postfault target hits, and the archive hash verifies. The schema should be normalized separately if automated table consumers require that key.
- This audit confirms evidence for the stated *bounded* original-JUnit campaigns. It does not establish that every possible exception site, interleaving, or cluster configuration was covered. The qualified SOLR-15/39/40 rows should not be described as 80 independent target-retaining runs.

## Recommendation on qualified rows

- **SOLR-15:** `quality_complete` is defensible for the *observed target-exception candidate*: the distinct original chaos JUnit applies the two native ZooKeeper faults, reaches the exact line-538 throw, repeats it on replay, and its original test and source classify the result as an expected protective abort. It is **not** defensible to describe the 160 shared main trials as 160 effective tests of this final guard; they yielded zero final-guard hits. No further run is needed for this candidate disposition. If a comparable 80-attempt fuzz budget specifically at this guard is required, reuse the unchanged `ShardSplitTest.testSplitWithChaosMonkey`, retain the exact parent-leader `/live_nodes/NODE` identity and `SplitShardCmd.split#L523` native `exists` hook, then admit only runs that revisit line 536 after the last fault. About 40–80 supplemental attempts would take roughly 1–2 hours at the observed 80–110 seconds per chaos run; this is a separate coverage experiment, not needed to classify the replayed abort.
- **SOLR-39/40:** Retaining `quality_complete` is defensible under their actual **80-attempt** stopping rule: both exact guards were reached in the healthy JUnit and in 6/21 fully applied postfault runs, their throwing arms were measured as untaken, and their source/test review separates expected old/future-document errors from the target oracle. The negative claim must remain limited to those 6/21 target-retaining runs. If the intended standard is 80 *target-retaining* runs per check, these rows do **not** meet it. A realistic supplemental run would prioritize observed `SolrZkClient` calls to `/aliases.json`, routed-alias properties and collection-state paths in the original four-Jetty `TimeRoutedAliasUpdateProcessorTest.test`, select them shortly before `lookupShardLeadersOfCollections:247` or `getAliasProps:133`, and keep only exact postfault guards for feedback. Another 80 targeted attempts would cost about 40 minutes at the observed 30 seconds per JUnit run; a cap of 240 attempts would be about two hours. If target retention remains low, report the actual effective count rather than claim an 80-effective budget.
