# ZooKeeper ended campaigns: same-occurrence fault continuation audit

Read-only audit, 2026-10-09 UTC. No JVM was launched and no campaign/checklist result was changed. Criterion: a `FAULT_APPLIED` event must precede a selected `TARGET_CHECK` before `REGION_EXIT`, with identical `node`, `epoch`, `occurrence`, and `thread_id`. This proves execution continued within that invocation; it does **not** alone prove the fault changed the selected predicate or caused a target exception.

## Requested `causal_precision_unverified` subset

I scanned every `events.jsonl.gz` in the ended **ZOOKEEPER-01**, **ZOOKEEPER-02**, and **ZOOKEEPER-03** campaigns (81 trial traces each, including healthy trial). Under the criterion above, **none** has a matching pair. The recorded fault often occurs in an unscoped quorum I/O occurrence and a later selected check occurs in a `SessionTrackerImpl` request occurrence; shared node or global sequence order cannot establish same-invocation continuation. Example: ZOOKEEPER-02 r0001 `FAULT_APPLIED` at seq 248 has `unscoped:zk2:1:root` on a `LearnerHandler` thread, while selected checks at seq 256/266/275 have `zk2:1:SessionTrackerImpl#checkSession(...):zk-request:2:6:*` on `ProcessThread`. ZOOKEEPER-03 r0001 has the same occurrence mismatch (fault seq 210, first selected check seq 223). ZOOKEEPER-01 r0001 has fault seq 187 in `unscoped:zk2:0:root`, while the later selected check seq 367 belongs to `zk2:1:SessionTrackerImpl#checkSession(...):zk-request:2:6:1`.

Pinned inputs: `data/mallory/cases/ZOOKEEPER-01/result.json` SHA-256 `afd9316d1c35926ba057cf44e94d035ec167e1ae21a827ba65a29cf8563829a8`; its `offline-causal-audit.json` SHA `2cd237781b1e3259fa85cdd329c37f75e219d43de794af75f7b39f52cd61ef5d`. `data/mallory/cases/ZOOKEEPER-02/full-campaign.tar.gz` SHA `14cc39e06435539d675a87959d579d433252dda78a21a3925028df45231281f9`; ZOOKEEPER-03 archive SHA `b8b5352743b7d11e10bb5192b57ca3217313fe4ddef8bc026bd82ca46a1e29a4`.
