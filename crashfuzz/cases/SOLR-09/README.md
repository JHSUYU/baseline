# SOLR-09 independent unit-cluster campaign

- Original Solr 8.11.4 workload: `DeleteReplicaTest.deleteReplicaAndVerifyDirectoryCleanup`, a four-node `SolrCloudTestCase` that creates `deletereplica_test`, deletes its leader replica, and checks directory cleanup.
- Selected production check: `DeleteReplicaCmd.deleteCore` at `solr/core/src/java/org/apache/solr/cloud/api/collections/DeleteReplicaCmd.java:278`, the synchronous `if (!callable.call())` branch that throws `SolrException("Could not remove replica")`. The probe evaluates `callable.call()` once, records the returned Boolean and target identity, and retains the original branch behavior.
- Fault scope: ZooKeeper reads/writes actually observed in this workload, selected by exact operation, path, production callsite, and hit ordinal. The graph-free feedback combines the runtime I/O identities with production JaCoCo coverage. A trial counts as valid only when every requested fault was matched and applied in order and the target check executed after the final fault.
- Budget: 80 valid trials, at most eight ordered faults, with an attempt cap of 240. Extend to 128 valid trials and ten faults only if one of the last eight valid trials adds production coverage or a stable I/O identity. Retain each raw event trace, JaCoCo execution file, JUnit log, elapsed time, and checkpoint.
- Healthy calibration: unchanged original JUnit passed in 13.918 seconds; the case-local instrumented JUnit passed in 14.909 seconds. The selected check was reached once. The run observed 322 relevant ZooKeeper I/O events across 63 exact operation/path identities before mutation. The independent campaign now runs in persistent tmux session `solr09_independent`, with `solr09_sync` updating the shared checklist after each result change.
- Selection adjustment at the atomic 31-trial checkpoint: 30 strict injections, 15 selected checks after the last fault, 15 valid trials, +1,301 production probes and +3 stable I/O identities. Four consecutive deep descendants lost the selected check and each took about 100 seconds. The campaign now interleaves an untried healthy root every two trials instead of every four while retaining multi-fault descendants. `result.json` records the before/after counts and coverage increments; all 31 completed trials retain their original plans and evidence.
- Second adjustment at the 37-trial checkpoint: trials 32–37 had six strict injections but zero retained targets and zero new production probes. Two timed out waiting for active collection, two timed out waiting for server responses, one failed to load collection state from ZooKeeper, and one had another JUnit failure before the selected check. On root-selection rounds the queue now prefers stable operation/path/caller identities whose previous single-fault trials retained the target; every fourth root-selection round still explores an untried I/O identity. The two policy changes and their separate before/after counters are persisted in `result.json`, while completed trial plans and raw evidence remain intact.
- Stage 1 finished and passed raw-trace audit: 219 attempts, 210 strictly applied plans, 128 valid trials with the selected check after the final fault, 5,472.519 seconds, +1,444 production JaCoCo probes and five stable I/O identities. The selected guard never threw. This is the archived `independent/result.json`; the combined case remains open for the separate HTTP/CoreAdmin phase.
- A clean replay of stage-1 trial 26 used its original three-fault plan. All three exact relative selectors matched and applied in order, but nondeterministic collection creation timed out before the selected check; the JUnit reported `Timeout occurred while waiting response from server`. The original trial reached the guard normally and later failed on a leader-registration retry. Neither run observed the selected `Could not remove replica` exception. Replay evidence is under `independent/evidence/replay-trial-026` and is excluded from fuzz attempt/valid counts.
- Stage 2 independently calibrates the same unchanged four-node JUnit and exact selected guard, then injects at the observed CoreAdmin `POST /admin/cores?action=UNLOAD&core=...` callsite as well as the observed ZooKeeper I/O. Its own budget is 80 valid trials/eight faults, conditionally 128/ten on late valid production coverage or stable I/O novelty. It has a separate checkpoint, traces and JaCoCo baseline under `http-stage2`; the combined result reports both phases and totals without adding coverage counts from distinct baselines.
- Source review for the stage-2 I/O family: `DeleteReplicaCmd.deleteCore` submits CoreAdmin `UNLOAD` through `ShardRequestTracker`; `ShardRequestor.call` captures an HTTP `IOException` as a shard response. Because `processResponses(..., false, ...)` does not abort on that response, `deleteCore` can still enqueue `DELETECORE` and wait for the replica to disappear from cluster state. The selected false branch needs both 30-second `waitForCoreNodeGone` checks to fail; an HTTP request failure or unrelated JUnit leader retry alone is not that outcome. The unchanged JUnit creates a two-replica collection, deletes its leader, and then checks leader replacement and directory cleanup.
- Stage 2's first mutation queue ended at 33 attempts and 16 valid trials. A checkpoint-only refill added 64 previously untried exact child plans from 16 successful, strictly injected parent traces; each candidate I/O occurred after its parent's last fault and before the same selected target. The saved receipt `http-stage2/evidence/campaign/refill-001-receipt.json` records every plan, source trace SHA-256, timing order, and the unique plan-set SHA-256 `21fb0ed5ee33946c3109c95710a3d31323ac4d42090d1f5bca874826b20a1953`. All earlier attempts and the 80-valid/eight-fault budget remain intact; the campaign resumed at attempt 34.

## Actor-aware V2 qualification

The two earlier phases selected ZooKeeper faults by path, production callsite, and ordinal, but did not distinguish the embedded nodes' `SolrZkClient` instances. They are retained as `legacy_coarse` evidence. Their valid counts do not count toward the high-quality result. In particular, the stage-2 trial 059 selected exception is an unconfirmed candidate: clean path/callsite replay reproduced it once, but the old trace cannot prove the two ZK faults affected the same Overseer reader as the selected guard.

`actor-v2` adds a case-local runtime client-to-node registration in `ZkController`. Just before the selected `DeleteReplicaCmd.deleteCore` command submits CoreAdmin `UNLOAD`, the probe binds its `OverseerCollectionMessageHandler.zkStateReader.getZkClient()` as `target_overseer`, plus the selected replica node/core as `target_replica_http`. The selected guard verifies it still uses that client object. A ZK selector now requires exact operation, resource, production caller, logical actor, and an ordinal within that identity; relative descendants also require the previous applied fault ID. Raw client object ID, node name, thread, and actual core remain in trace for audit. The raw node name includes a temporary Jetty port and is never used as a cross-run selector.

The unchanged original four-node JUnit passed twice (11.902s, 14.983s), and the actor-instrumented JUnit passed twice (13.961s, 14.959s). Each instrumented run registered four node clients, bound one Overseer client, and reached the selected guard once with the same bound client. The original test chose different target cores and Overseer client IDs across the two runs. ZK callbacks on other threads retained the bound `target_overseer` client identity. `actor-v2/evidence/healthy-actor-audit.json` records the trace hashes and identity checks.

The independent V2 campaign runs in persistent tmux `solr09_actor_v2`; `solr09_actor_v2_sync` updates the case row. Its budget is 80 strict, post-final-target trials with at most eight ordered faults and 240 attempts, extending to 128/ten only on production coverage or stable actor/caller/operation/resource I/O novelty among the last eight valid trials. The root `result.json` top-level trial, time, target, and coverage fields represent **V2 only**; earlier phases are in `legacy_coarse`. Each V2 trial retains events, JaCoCo, JUnit log, elapsed time, the exact plan, and its atomic checkpoint.

An independent, non-budget role-relative diagnostic of old trial 059 ran in tmux `solr09_actor_replay059`. Two healthy traces show the order `target_replica_http UNLOAD → target_overseer StateWatcher GET_CHILDREN_STAT → target_overseer fetchCollectionState GET_DATA → selected guard` on the **same** client object. This diagnostic uses a new logical-role plan, distinct from trial 059's absolute path/callsite plan. Five of six runs applied all three faults and kept the target; runs 02 and 06 hit the selected exception. A separate diagnostic copied V2 classes and read actual ZooKeeper state **after** the original guard Boolean was fixed: strict run 04 hit the selected exception although the same Overseer client read server `state.json` version 8 with the replica absent. These runs are excluded from the V2 fuzz denominator. Details and raw paths are in `actor-v2/CANDIDATE-059.md`.

The coarse HTTP stage stopped after its atomic trial-108 checkpoint, with `pause-request.json` and `pause-ack.json` recording the next exact plan and `jvm_started=false`. Its next `trial-109` directory does not exist. After V2 completed, the obsolete paused controller and child Python process were stopped at that pre-JVM boundary; all 108 completed coarse trials and their raw evidence remain intact. The root result records this under `legacy_coarse`.

Independent non-budget diagnostic run 08 used a separate read-only `ZkStateReader` timeout hook. Both 30-second waits saw an active collection watcher but no cached collection state before their cleanup. The selected guard still threw after ZK had removed the replica and after the target core's instance/data directories were absent. The upstream SOLR-16257 fix changes the map representation, but its race symptom overlaps this result, so novelty remains unresolved; the root result marks a candidate, not a new bug. The diagnostic raw trace, original JUnit log, directory snapshot, and source comparison are documented in `actor-v2/CANDIDATE-059.md`. After V2 completed, a separate measured-load replay stopped on its third attempt with a strict three-fault, post-final-target selected exception at pre-run host load 7.13/16 cores. Its raw trace proves the same bound Overseer client at both ZK injections and the guard. That replay is excluded from the main fuzz budget and its exact evidence is in the candidate report.

### V2 completed-budget result

The actor-aware main campaign terminated at its extended 128-valid-trial budget after **161 attempts, 130 strictly matched and applied fault sequences, and 128 trials with the selected check after the final fault**. It ran for **3,063.996 seconds** (51 minutes 3.996 seconds). All plans had at most ten ordered faults. The main campaign saw **zero selected exceptions**; the separate diagnostic reproductions above are never added to its denominator. Its feedback gained 281 production JaCoCo probes and 18 stable actor/caller/operation/resource I/O identities relative to healthy calibration. The budget extension from 80/eight to 128/ten was recorded at valid trials 73–80 when strict, target-retaining runs added production coverage or stable I/O identities.

`actor-v2/evidence/campaign/raw-audit.json` recomputes every trial from its preserved trace and receipt. It found zero discrepancies across all 161 attempts, including the order of exact matched/applied faults, relative ordinals, the concrete Overseer client and selected replica node at the target, post-final-fault target reach, JUnit exit, and required JaCoCo files. `coverage-summary.json` merges only the 128 valid JaCoCo files with two instrumented healthy runs: covered Solr production methods increased from 3,949 to 3,979 and branches from 4,662 to 4,793 (+30 methods, +131 branches). `DeleteReplicaCmd.deleteCore` remains at 6/16 covered branches in the main campaign, consistent with the zero selected exceptions there. The shared checklist records `quality_complete` for this **methodological** campaign and keeps candidate novelty separately at `actor_aware_reproduced_known_overlap_novelty_unresolved`.
