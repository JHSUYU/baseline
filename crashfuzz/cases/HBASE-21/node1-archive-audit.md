# HBASE-21 node1 raw archive

`node1-work-raw.tar.zst` contains the complete HBASE-21 `work` directory from node1, including the 144-attempt campaign logs, original JUnit records, checkpoints, JaCoCo `.exec` files, coverage reports, and the separate original-test manifest supplement. `node1-work-raw-receipt.json` records the archive SHA-256 and a SHA-256 tree digest of all 1,976 source files. Every extracted file's size and SHA-256 matched the still-preserved node1 source.
